How to delete your data

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · OnHighTech Solutions (website and WhatsApp)

You can ask OnHighTech Solutions to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time, free of charge. This applies to our website onhightech.co.zw and our WhatsApp service on +263 78 157 6825. Our Privacy Policy explains what data we hold and why.

1. How to request deletion

If you used our WhatsApp service:

  1. Open WhatsApp on the phone number you used with us.
  2. Send the message DELETE MY DATA to +263 78 157 6825.
  3. We reply to confirm we received your request.
Send “DELETE MY DATA” on WhatsApp

If you have an OnHighTech account on our website (email or Google sign-in):

  1. Send DELETE MY ACCOUNT and your account’s email address to +263 78 157 6825 on WhatsApp.
  2. We email that address to confirm the request comes from the account owner.
  3. Reply to that email (or follow its instructions) to confirm.
Send “DELETE MY ACCOUNT” on WhatsApp

If you signed in with Google: after deletion you can also remove OnHighTech from your Google Account (Google Account → Security → Third-party connections).

We only act on requests we can verify, so that nobody else can delete your data. We never ask for your password.

2. What we delete

  • Your website account: name, email address, password, linked WhatsApp number and Google sign-in link.
  • Your WhatsApp chat data: your number, profile name, messages and form answers, and your chat history with us.
  • Your CV details and any CV files you sent us.
  • Your interview practice access and progress.
  • Saved bill details (such as account, meter or smartcard numbers) and receipt messages, except as below.

3. What we may keep, and why

Data Why How long
Payment and transaction records (amount, date, method, references) Accounting, tax and fraud prevention laws, and resolving disputes Up to 7 years, then deleted
A record that you asked for deletion To show we acted on your request Up to 12 months

Data held by others under their own policies is not controlled by us: for example your messages in WhatsApp itself (see WhatsApp’s privacy settings), your mobile money or bank records, and records kept by billers and bus operators.

4. How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of a verified request and confirm on WhatsApp or by email when it is done. Copies in our secure backups are overwritten in our normal backup cycle within a further 30 days.

5. Other choices

  • Stop messages: stop replying or block our WhatsApp number at any time.
  • Unlink WhatsApp or Google from your account without deleting it, or get a copy of your data, or correct it: contact us the same way.
  • If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ).
