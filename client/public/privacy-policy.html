Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Applies to onhightech.co.zw and our WhatsApp services

OnHighTech Solutions (“OnHighTech”, “we”, “us”) provides career tools and everyday services in Zimbabwe: interview practice, CV help, bus booking and bill payments (airtime, ZESA, DStv and other billers), on our website onhightech.co.zw and through our WhatsApp number +263 78 157 6825. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have, including how to delete your data.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details (website): your name, email address and password (stored only in a protected, hashed form we cannot read). Optionally, a WhatsApp number to link your account to our WhatsApp chat.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, Google shares your name, email address and a Google account identifier with us. We do not receive your Google password.
  • CV and career information: details you provide or upload for your CV, such as your name, contact details, education, work history, skills, certificates and any CV file you send.
  • Interview practice: the topics you open and buy, and your progress.
  • Bus booking: passenger names, contact numbers and trip details needed for a ticket.
  • Bill payments: the biller, your account, meter, smartcard or phone number with that biller, the amount, and the result (for example ZESA tokens, receipts and references). The biller may return your account name and address so you can confirm them before paying.
  • Payment details: the mobile money number you pay with (EcoCash or OneMoney) and payment references. Card numbers are entered on our payment providers’ secure pages; we never see or store your full card details.
  • Messages to us: what you send when you contact us for help.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information: your IP address, browser and device type, and the times you use our services, recorded in server logs for security and troubleshooting.
  • Browser storage: small items saved in your browser to keep you signed in and make the site work without data; see Browser storage.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the services you ask for: interview practice, CVs, bus tickets and bill payments.
  • To create and secure your account, including sending verification and password-reset codes to your email.
  • To take payments, confirm them, and deliver what you paid for (for example unlocking a topic or sending your ZESA token).
  • To send you receipts and service messages by SMS, email or WhatsApp, such as tokens and payment confirmations.
  • To help you when you contact us, and to fix problems.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep records required by law, and resolve disputes.
  • To improve our services, using information in summary form where possible.

We process your information because it is needed to provide the service you requested (our agreement with you), because you consented (for example to link your WhatsApp number or sign in with Google), to meet legal obligations (such as financial records), and for our legitimate interests in keeping our services secure and working well.

3. Our WhatsApp services

When you message our WhatsApp number, we receive your WhatsApp phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and the messages and answers you send us (including in WhatsApp forms). We use them only to reply to you and provide the service you chose in the chat. Messages are carried by WhatsApp, a Meta service, under WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy. We send you messages only in reply to you or about a service you used with us (for example a payment confirmation or a code you asked for). You can stop at any time by not replying, blocking our number, or asking us to delete your data.

4. Who we share your information with

We share personal information only as needed to run our services, with:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Payment providers (Paynow and Pesepay): to process your EcoCash, OneMoney and card payments.
  • Paynow BillPay and the billers you pay (for example ZETDC, mobile networks, MultiChoice, councils, schools, insurers): to check your account and deliver your payment.
  • SMS provider (SMS Localhost): to send receipt and token SMS to your phone.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”, to sign you in.
  • AI service providers (such as OpenAI and DeepSeek): the CV information you provide may be processed by these providers to help write and improve your CV. We send only what is needed for that task.
  • Email provider: to send verification and password-reset codes.
  • Our hosting provider: our servers and databases are hosted by a cloud provider (Contabo).
  • Bus operators: the passenger details needed to issue and check your ticket.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or OnHighTech.

These providers may use your information only to provide their service to us, under their own privacy terms.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Paynow and Pesepay. When you pay by EcoCash or OneMoney, we pass your mobile money number and the amount to the payment provider, which asks you to approve the payment on your phone. Card payments are completed on the provider’s secure page. We keep the payment reference, amount, method, date and outcome so we can confirm your purchase and help with any problem.

6. How long we keep your information

  • Account and service information: while your account is open or you keep using our services, and up to 12 months after your last use, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Payment and transaction records: up to 7 years, as needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention and disputes.
  • CV files and CV details: until you delete them or ask us to, or 12 months after your last use.
  • Codes and short-lived data: verification and reset codes expire within 30 minutes; server logs are kept for a limited time for security.

7. How we protect your information

We use HTTPS encryption on our website, store passwords only as salted hashes, sign session tokens, limit repeated sign-in attempts, encrypt WhatsApp form data in transit, and restrict access to personal information to people and systems that need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

8. Your rights

Under Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and other applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information (see below);
  • object to or ask us to limit certain uses, and withdraw consent you gave (for example unlinking your WhatsApp number or Google sign-in);
  • complain to the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ), the data protection authority.

To use these rights, contact us. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

9. How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete your personal information at any time (full steps on our data deletion page), in either of these ways:

  1. On WhatsApp: send DELETE MY DATA to +263 78 157 6825 (open WhatsApp) from the number you used with us.
  2. For a website account: message us on the same WhatsApp number with DELETE MY ACCOUNT and the email address of your account. We will email that address to confirm the request comes from you.

We will delete your account, profile, CV details and files, chat history and linked information within 30 days and confirm when it is done. We may keep payment and transaction records for as long as the law requires (see section 6); these are then deleted. If you signed in with Google, you can also remove OnHighTech’s access in your Google Account settings.

10. Browser storage

Our website saves a few items in your browser’s local storage: your sign-in session, your choice between the website and WhatsApp, and saved copies of pages and interview content so the site opens with little or no data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you sign in with Google, Google may set its own cookies on the sign-in window. You can clear these items at any time in your browser settings; you will then be signed out.

11. Where your information is processed

Some of our providers (such as Meta, Google, our hosting provider and AI providers) may store or process information outside Zimbabwe. When this happens, we rely on these providers’ security and data protection commitments and share only what is needed for the service.

12. Children

Our services are intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and for important changes we will tell you on our website or in our WhatsApp chat.
